About POWRNET

POWRNET is a Kenyan digital brand based in Nairobi, Kenya.

We build simple, trusted digital products — websites, mobile apps, and content — that help people in Kenya learn, earn, and grow online.

Our mission is: Powering Kenya with digital opportunity.

What We Do

  • Build websites and apps for Kenyans
  • Create useful, safe and easy-to-use digital tools
  • Share knowledge about earning, technology and business online

POWRNET is owned and operated in Kenya.
